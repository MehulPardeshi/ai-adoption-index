## Scope of the October 27 decisions (Bloomberg superseded above)

- **Access** covers ordinary manual reading, within applicable access rules. It does not authorize automated collection, bulk downloading, text and data mining, or uploading documents to external AI services such as DeepSeek.
- **Local storage** refers to copies of the source documents. A conditional decision applies only when its stated condition is satisfied. Personal research-copy permission does not automatically cover a shared project corpus. Preserve attribution and copyright notices, restrict access, and follow the project retention policy. Public availability or absence of a copyright notice does not by itself establish permission to copy or redistribute.
- **Team sharing** approves source links or citations and independently written notes. It does not approve sharing transcript files, substantial verbatim extracts, or notes that reproduce or reconstruct the source text. Permission to share full text remains unresolved.
- **Publication** for public company-website sources covers source metadata and original analysis, including labels or scores that do not reproduce source text. Quotations require separate review; full transcripts and substantial extracts are excluded. Publication approval does not independently authorize an otherwise uncleared collection or processing method.
- **Bloomberg** is a subscription-terminal source, not a publicly accessible webpage source. Citation and original-commentary publication are approved within the stated scope; permission to release derived datasets, labels, or scores remains to be confirmed against the applicable license.
- **Missing documents** are unassessed candidates, not rejected sources and not evidence of permission. Assess a specific document if one is subsequently identified.
