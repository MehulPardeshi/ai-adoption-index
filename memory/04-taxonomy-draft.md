## Process the brief requires
1. Pull a sample of **~30–50 real documents** (mix of sectors and document types).
2. Read them and note how firms actually describe AI use.
3. **Derive** categories from what you see. Compare against the starter list below only *afterward*.
4. Formalize: for each category, write **inclusion rules, exclusion rules, and 2–3 example sentences from real filings** (cited or paraphrased, not scraped verbatim into the report).
