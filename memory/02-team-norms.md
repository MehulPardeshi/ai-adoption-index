# 02 — Team Norms (as signed)

> Source: the signed team contract (signed 09/20–09/24/2026 by all five members). **These norms are authoritative. Do not invent alternatives.** Changes require a majority vote, logged in [`09-decisions-log.md`](09-decisions-log.md).

## Meetings
- **Weekly, virtual:**
  - **Thursdays 2:00–3:00 pm:** client meeting (with Julie Agnew)
  - **Sundays 8:00–9:00 pm:** internal team meeting
- **Missing a meeting:** give **24 hours' notice** unless it's an emergency, and **post a progress update in the group chat** instead.

## Check-ins
- **WhatsApp group chat.**
- **Respond within 3 hours** with a message or reaction, or as soon as possible.

## Decision-making
- **Majority vote.**

## Work distribution: two complementary systems
| System | What it's for | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| **Shared spreadsheet** (task / owner / deadline) | High-level tracker of who owns what and by when. **This is the one the contract mandates.** | **Source of truth for ownership and deadlines** |
| **GitHub Issues + Project board** | Granular engineering backlog: technical tasks and bugs that come up during the build. Seeded from the 6-week timeline. | Sits *underneath* the spreadsheet. The issue owner is "TBD" until the spreadsheet says otherwise. |

Details and the spreadsheet link: [`docs/TASK_TRACKER.md`](../docs/TASK_TRACKER.md).
(The contract names only the spreadsheet. GitHub Issues were added because the course requires a GitHub kanban board.)

## Conflict resolution
1. **First missed expectation:** it goes on the backlog, the group is told, and it's **due by the next sprint**. The member **gives an explanation** so the team understands why.
2. **Second miss with no explanation to the group:** **escalate to the mentor TA** (Hengkai Zheng). It is then also reflected in the **peer evaluation**.

(Course policy, from the syllabus: if the team can't resolve an issue, contact the TA. Removing a member requires a unanimous vote of the others, after the TA process.)
